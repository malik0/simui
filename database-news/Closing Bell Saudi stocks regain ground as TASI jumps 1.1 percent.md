## Story

JEDDAH: Saudi Arabia’s Tadawul All Share Index rebounded on Sunday, gaining 112.87 points, or 1.09 percent, to close at 10,505.84 after four consecutive sessions of declines.

Trading activity totaled about 109.01 million shares worth more than SR2.26 billion ($602.7 million), with 220 stocks advancing and 41 declining.

The Kingdom’s parallel market, Nomu, rose 175.98 points, or 0.84 percent, to close at 21,248.86, with 47 stocks advancing and 20 declining. The MSCI Tadawul 30 Index gained 16.25 points, or 1.16 percent, to close at 1,416.5.

Raydan Food Co. was the main market’s top performer, with its share price rising 9.97 percent to SR18.97. Abdullah Saad Abu Moati for Bookstores Co. gained 7.69 percent to SR32.78, while Armah Sports Co. advanced 6.69 percent to SR65.35.
