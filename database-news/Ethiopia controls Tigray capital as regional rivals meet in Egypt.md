# Ethiopia controls Tigray capital as regional rivals meet in Egypt

![Arab News](images/65d7304b4a6e.jpg)

- Ethiopian forces have taken control of the Tigray capital, fueling fears of renewed conflict, as regional leaders convene in Egypt to address potential escalation.

## Story

Addis Ababa: Ethiopian federal forces were in full control of the Tigray rebels’ regional capital, a local journalist said Sunday, while Ethiopia’s regional rivals gathered in Egypt to discuss the risk of a wider war.

The Tigray People’s Liberation Front (TPLF) launched attacks against federal forces on September 23, sparking fears of a return to the devastating civil war of 2020-2022.

The presidents of Egypt, Somalia, Sudan and Eritrea -- all of whom have difficult relations with Ethiopia -- were set to meet in the Egyptian city of Alamein on Sunday to discuss the conflict.

Ethiopia has accused Eritrea of directly backing the Tigray rebels, with the added support of Sudan and Egypt, which all three countries deny.
