- Polls have just opened in Brazil, for one of the most tightly contested elections in the country's history.
- The incumbent president, Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva, is running for a fourth non-consecutive term.
- His main rival is far-right senator, Flavio Bolsonaro, son of the former president, Jair Bolsonaro.
