# Saudi and Moroccan Derbies take Kings League MENA centerstage as Ultra

![Arab News](images/9761fce510d9.jpg)

- In Matchday 5 of the Kings League MENA held in Riyadh, Ultra Chmicha remained unbeaten with a 5-3 win over Bakhira FC in the highly anticipated Morocco Derby, while the Saudi Derby saw DR7 narrowly defeat RA2 7-6 in a thrilling contest.
- With this victory, Ultra Chmicha leads the league standings with five wins, followed by Kuwait’s FWZ with four wins and DR7 along with Jordan’s Red Zone holding three wins each.

## Story

RIYADH: Ilyas Elmaliki’s Ultra Chmicha won the Morocco Derby, while Drb7h’s DR7 continued their resurgence by winning an all-Saudi showdown on a Matchday 5 of the Kings League MENA at Cool Arena in Riyadh.

The Saudi derby between defending champions DR7 and RA2 delivered one of Matchday 5’s most dramatic finishes, with DR7 getting their third straight win with a thrilling 7-6 victory.

RA2, led by Team President Raed, took the early advantage as Eloy Amoedo scored twice to put his side in front, before DR7, led by Team President Drb7h, responded with a spectacular goal from Nawaf Arwan to keep the defending champions within reach. Drb7h was unable to convert his President Penalty, while Raed found the net from the spot as the momentum continued to shift between the two sides. With the match entering Matchball Mode, a VAR check brought the score level at 6-6, setting up the decisive final moments before DR7’s star attacker Abdullah Alaqueeli scored the winning goal to claim victory in a pulsating Saudi derby.

Saudi Arabia’s Rising Peaks, led by Team President Abdulrahman Al-Shehri, secured their second consecutive victory on Saturday, defeating Egypt’s Turbo — led by Team President Tarboun — 5-2 to continue their climb up the standings.
