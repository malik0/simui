## Story

RIYADH: Saudi Arabia’s licensed tourism hospitality facilities rose 17.9 percent in the second quarter as the Kingdom continued to expand accommodation capacity, even as hotel occupancy and room rates declined.&nbsp;

The number of licensed facilities reached 6,278 in the quarter, up from 5,326 a year earlier, according to the General Authority for Statistics . Serviced apartments and other hospitality facilities accounted for 3,258 establishments, or 51.9 percent of the total, while hotels numbered 3,020.&nbsp;

Strengthening the hospitality sector is a key part of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030 economic diversification drive as the Kingdom develops its tourism industry. The Kingdom surpassed its original target of 100 million annual tourists in 2023, seven years ahead of schedule, and has since raised its 2030 target to 150 million domestic and inbound tourists.&nbsp;

In its latest report, GASTAT stated: “The supply of licensed rooms continued to expand significantly, with the number of licensed hotel rooms increasing by 13.2 percent, while the number of rooms in serviced apartments and other hospitality facilities increased by 19.7 percent, compared to the same period in 2025.” &nbsp;
