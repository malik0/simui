# She died covering war for Fox News. Her parents blame the network — and

![NPR](images/c9e73cde92d1.jpg)

- The family members of two Fox News journalists killed while covering the war in Ukraine are suing the network, saying it put getting the story ahead of its people.

## Story

When the Fox News Channel hosted hundreds of advertisers and industry executives at the ornate Hammerstein Ballroom in Manhattan two years ago, the network centered attention on a tragic yet heroic figure: the war correspondent Benjamin Hall.

"From Ukraine to the Middle East, and here at home, the dedicated journalists of Fox News are trying to inform Americans, even in the most perilous of circumstances," Hall proclaimed .

Fox News correspondent Benjamin Hall is treated for injuries that he sustained while reporting in Ukraine in March 2022. Fox News / Screenshot by NPR hide caption

Hall knows peril all too well. In the first weeks of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine, a strike by Russian forces hit the car in which Hall's team had been traveling outside Kyiv.
