# Head-Mounted Display

A head-mounted display (HMD) is the headset worn for [[Virtual Reality]]. It combines small screens, optics and sensors.

- Optics are usually [[Fresnel Lens]] or pancake lenses that make a close screen focus comfortably.
- Lens spacing should match the user's [[Interpupillary Distance]].
- Sensors enable [[Inside-Out Tracking]] and [[Six Degrees of Freedom]] pose.
- Determines the [[Field of View]] and the maximum refresh rate, see [[Frame Rate]].
- Paired with [[VR Controllers]] or [[Hand Tracking]] for input.
- Standalone headsets run apps on-device; tethered ones use a PC.
