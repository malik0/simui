# Inside-Out Tracking

Cameras on the [[Head-Mounted Display]] observe the room and compute the headset's position using SLAM (simultaneous localization and mapping), often fused with an inertial sensor (IMU).

- Needs no external base stations, unlike outside-in systems.
- Provides [[Six Degrees of Freedom]] for the headset and for [[VR Controllers]].
- The same cameras enable [[Hand Tracking]].
- Can lose tracking in dark or featureless rooms.
- IMU data keeps [[Latency]] low between camera frames.
