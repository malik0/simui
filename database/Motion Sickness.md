# Motion Sickness

Also called cybersickness. It is thought to come from a mismatch between visual motion and the vestibular system, which senses real movement.

Common triggers:
- High [[Latency]] or a low [[Frame Rate]].
- Artificial locomotion such as smooth stick movement. Teleporting and vignetting reduce it.
- Wide [[Field of View]] with strong peripheral motion.
- Incorrect [[Interpupillary Distance]] settings.
- Camera movement the user does not control.

Mitigations: fixed reference points like a cockpit, snap turning, and honoring [[Six Degrees of Freedom]] head motion one-to-one.
