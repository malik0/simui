# VR Controllers

Handheld devices tracked in 3D space that give buttons, triggers, thumbsticks and haptic feedback.

- Tracked with [[Six Degrees of Freedom]], usually by cameras on the [[Head-Mounted Display]] ([[Inside-Out Tracking]]).
- In [[WebXR]] they appear as input sources with a grip pose and a target ray pose.
- In [[Three.js]], `renderer.xr.getController(i)` returns an object you add to the [[Scene Graph]].
- Alternative to [[Hand Tracking]].
