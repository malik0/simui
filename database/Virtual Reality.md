# Virtual Reality

Virtual reality (VR) replaces the user's view of the world with a computer-generated, interactive environment.

- Delivered mainly through a [[Head-Mounted Display]].
- Immersion depends on [[Six Degrees of Freedom]] tracking, low [[Latency]] and a high [[Frame Rate]].
- Each eye gets its own image, see [[Stereoscopic Rendering]].
- Poor tuning causes [[Motion Sickness]].
- On the web it is exposed through [[WebXR]].
- Ivan Sutherland's "Sword of Damocles" (1968) is commonly cited as the first head-mounted VR/AR system.
